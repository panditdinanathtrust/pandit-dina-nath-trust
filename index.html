Who We Are

About the Trust

Pandit Dina Nath Charitable Trust is established as a public charitable trust and a non-government, non-profit making body. Its work is guided by the objectives set out in its Trust Deed.

The Trust’s principal office is stated in its deed at Ward No. 06, near Anaj Mandi, Balaji Nagar, Farrukhnagar, Gurugram, Haryana.

Our guiding belief

We believe that access to healthcare, learning opportunities, dignity and practical community support can strengthen families and communities.

Our motto: Service to Humanity

What We Stand For

Our Charitable Objectives

Based on the objects described in the Trust Deed.

⚕️

Healthcare & Medical Relief

Supporting hospitals, dispensaries, medical clinics, nursing homes, diagnostic facilities and medical relief for people in need.

📚

Education & Scholarships

Establishing or supporting educational institutions, libraries, research, technical education, scholarships and fellowships.

🤝

Community Welfare

Relief and support for poor, needy, elderly, widowed, disabled and disadvantaged persons and families.

🌱

Rural Development

Promoting rural development, sanitation, clean surroundings, community facilities and public welfare initiatives.

🐾

Animal Welfare

Supporting animal care, shelter, treatment, awareness and welfare activities.

🧘

Health & Well-being

Promoting awareness, yoga, meditation, preventive health and well-being initiatives for the community.
